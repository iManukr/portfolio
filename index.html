MinuteMan

Meeting notes are where commitments get lost. MinuteMan is an agentic pipeline that turns a transcript or recorded video into owned, dated action items, with a person approving everything before it reaches the team.

  • Extracts owner, task, deadline and the exact source quote, so every item can be checked.
  • Approval is a real interrupt in the LangGraph execution graph.
  • Scored against a labelled dataset with precision, recall and F1.
  • Works with Claude or GPT, pulls YouTube captions, posts approved output to Slack.
